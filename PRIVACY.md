# Privacy Policy for AI Guardrails Extension

**Last Updated:** December 29, 2025

## Overview
AI Guardrails ("the Extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This extension operates with a privacy-first approach: **all data stays on your device**.

## Data Collection
The Extension collects the following data **locally on your device only**:

### User Activity Data
- Intervention events (when a guardrail is triggered)
- User actions (which alternative you chose: journal, talk, wait, or continue)
- Strike counts per category (resets daily)
- 24-hour block timestamps

### Website Content Analysis
- The Extension analyzes the text you type in ChatGPT/Claude.ai messages **locally** to detect emotional keywords
- **No message content is stored** - only metadata (timestamp, category triggered, strike level)

### User-Generated Content
- Journal entries you create within the Extension
- User preferences (which guardrail categories are enabled)

## Data Storage
- **All data is stored locally** using Chrome's Storage API
- **Nothing is transmitted** to external servers
- **No telemetry or analytics** are collected
- **No account or sign-up required**

## Data Usage
Data is used solely to:
- Track your self-imposed guardrails and strike counts
- Display usage statistics in your dashboard
- Save your journal entries for personal reference
- Remember your category preferences

## Data Sharing
**We do not share, sell, or transfer any data to third parties.** All data remains on your device.

## Your Control
You have full control over your data:
- Export all data as JSON or TXT files anytime
- Delete all data with one click in Settings
- Disable or uninstall the Extension at any time (all data is removed)

## Host Permissions
The Extension requires access to:
- chat.openai.com
- chatgpt.com
- claude.ai

These permissions are used **only** to intercept your messages before sending, analyze them locally for keywords, and display intervention modals. We do not access, store, or transmit your conversations.

## Data Security
All data is stored securely in Chrome's local storage on your device. Since no data is transmitted externally, there is no risk of data breaches from our servers (we don't have any servers).

## Third-Party Services
The Extension does **not** integrate with any third-party services, analytics platforms, or external APIs. It operates entirely offline within your browser.

## Children's Privacy
The Extension does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. The Extension is intended for general audiences and does not target children.

## Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy occasionally to reflect changes in the Extension's functionality. Changes will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date above. Continued use of the Extension after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

## Contact
For questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue at:
https://github.com/Issac-Lopez/ai-guardrails-extension/issues

## Legal Basis for Data Processing (GDPR Compliance)
For users in the European Economic Area (EEA):
- Data processing is based on your **consent** (by installing and using the Extension)
- All data is processed **locally on your device** and never leaves your browser
- You can withdraw consent at any time by uninstalling the Extension

## Your Rights
You have the right to:
- Access your data (use the Export feature in Settings)
- Delete your data (use the Clear All Data button in Settings)
- Withdraw consent (uninstall the Extension)

---

**TL;DR:** Everything stays on your device. We collect nothing externally. We share nothing. You control everything. No servers, no tracking, no BS.
